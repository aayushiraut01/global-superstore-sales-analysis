# Global Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

The analysis was performed using Python and Pandas with data visualization to identify meaningful business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Compare sales and profitability across categories and regions
- Identify high- and low-performing sub-categories
- Identify top and bottom-performing products
- Analyze the relationship between discount and profit
- Analyze the relationship between sales and profit
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Calculate and compare profit margins

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Analysis Performed

### Data Cleaning
- Converted sales values into numeric format
- Converted order and shipping dates into datetime format
- Checked data types and data consistency
- Verified duplicate and missing records

### Exploratory Data Analysis
- Overall sales, profit, order count, and average sales
- Category-wise sales and profit analysis
- Region-wise sales and profit analysis
- Sub-category profitability analysis
- Product-level sales and profitability analysis
- Profit margin analysis
- Discount vs profit analysis
- Sales vs profit analysis
- Monthly sales trend analysis

## Key Business Insights

- Total sales were approximately 12.64 million, with total profit of approximately 1.47 million.
- Technology generated the highest total sales and total profit among the three main categories.
- Furniture generated strong sales but comparatively lower profit, indicating weaker profitability.
- Central had the highest total sales and total profit among the regions.
- Southeast Asia generated relatively high sales but comparatively low profit, indicating a potential profitability concern.
- Copiers were the highest-profit sub-category.
- Tables generated substantial sales but recorded an overall loss, making them an important area for further investigation.

## Conclusion

The analysis provides an overview of sales and profitability across different business dimensions. The findings highlight strong-performing categories and products as well as areas where profitability could be improved.

The project demonstrates practical use of Python and Pandas for exploratory data analysis and business-oriented decision making.
